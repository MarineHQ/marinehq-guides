For a fibreglass motor yacht **up to 50 ft**, the clearest number on the Gold Coast is Gold Coast City Marina's package: **from $89.50 a foot**, haul-out to relaunch, about $4,475 for a 50-footer. **Above 50 ft, every yard quotes.** Build it from the published parts and a 60 ft motor yacht lands around **$6,700** for antifoul, Propspeed and anodes, and closer to **$9,000** once you add the hull polish most owners want while she is out. Here is what is in the job, what each line costs, and what to check before you sign.

The per-foot number is real, but it describes the paint job. On a 60 ft motor yacht the running gear, the anodes, the seacocks and the polish are where the rest of the bill and most of the value sit.

## What is actually in the job

<ol class="steps"><li><b>Haul-out.</b> Travelift from the water to the hardstand. The Coomera yards run 300-tonne lifts; Runaway Bay's stops at 16 m.</li><li><b>Waterblast and environmental levy.</b> High-pressure wash on a wash-down pad that captures the run-off. The levy is the yard's cost of doing that legally.</li><li><b>Inspection.</b> Hull, existing paint, anodes, skin fittings, props, shafts, rudders and seals. This is the moment to find the problem, not after relaunch.</li><li><b>Preparation.</b> Wet sand, never dry sand or burn off; mask the waterline; prime any bare patches.</li><li><b>Paint.</b> Two full coats, a third on the waterline, chines, rudders and leading edges.</li><li><b>Running gear.</b> Propspeed on props, shafts and rudders; new anodes; prop polish if wanted.</li><li><b>Extras while she is out.</b> Hull cut and polish, seacock service, skeg bearings, moisture checks.</li><li><b>Relaunch.</b> Micron One has to be back in the water within one to three months; Micron Extra 2 will wait up to 12.</li></ol>

## The extras, line by line

**Propspeed.** A silicone foul-release coating on the props, shafts and rudders. Reported Australian shipwright quotes sit around $850 for a twin-prop set once the labour is honest, with cheaper quotes of $450 that the trade says undercharge; the New Zealand benchmark for a twin-prop 13 m boat is NZ$1,200. Two 500 ml kits are about $478 in materials alone. Lasts a year at minimum and up to three. Watson Marine at Coomera is the approved applicator and also scans, repitches and polishes props.

**Anodes.** Shaft collars are $25 to $65 each, hull blocks about $38, so $250 to $400 in parts for a twin-shaft boat with a few hull anodes. Fitting labour is not published anywhere and is usually bundled.

**Hull cut and polish.** Harbourforce's two package prices imply $35 a foot for a waterline-to-gunwale cut and polish, so $2,100 on a 60-footer. Doing it on the hardstand is cheaper and better than doing it in the water.

**Seacocks, skin fittings, skeg bearings.** Offered as quote items by the shipwrights. Not published. Have them exercised and inspected every haul-out regardless.

**Moisture and blister checks.** No Australian pricing published. Ask; on an older hull it is worth more than the paint.

<div class="callout orange" markdown="1"><span class="eyebrow">Cost trap</span>
A "from $X a foot" quote is a paint quote. Ask in writing whether it includes the lift both ways, the waterblast and levy, the number of hardstand days, the paint grade and litres, Propspeed, anodes and the polish. If it does not say, it is not included.</div>

How much: wetted area is roughly length times beam times 0.85. A Maritimo M60 at 18.53 m by 5.23 m is about 82 m². With Micron Extra 2 by roller at 9 m² a litre, two coats and a stripe coat is about **20 litres, two 10 L tins, $1,696**. By spray the coverage halves and the litres double. With Micron One at 5.4 m² a litre it is about 30 litres, three tins, $1,745. Spraying costs more paint, not less; it buys a better finish.

<div class="callout" markdown="1"><span class="eyebrow">Hard or soft?</span>
Ablative or self-polishing paints wear away as the boat moves and suit boats that are used. Hard antifouls need scrubbing and suit boats that sit. For a motor yacht that goes out, ablative. For an aluminium hull, copper-free, always.</div>

## How often, and what fouling costs you

The local applicators say once a year in Gold Coast water. Nautilus, the insurer, says every 12 to 18 months regardless of size. The Boat Works says six to fifteen months. Brisbane yards put frequently used powerboats at six to twelve months and only dry-stored boats at 18 to 24. The national biofouling guidelines add that coatings should be renewed towards the shorter end of their rated life, and that you should keep the paint invoices and slipway receipts.

The reason to keep to it is fuel. An IMO study found slime as thin as half a millimetre over half the hull adds 20% to 25% to fuel burn, and a light layer of barnacles up to 55%. A 2025 KTH study measured slime alone raising the power needed by up to 18%. Those are ship figures, but the physics is the same on a 60-footer burning 180 litres an hour. A season on a fouled hull can cost more in diesel than the antifoul did.

## The rules

In-water hull scraping is generally not allowed in Australia without permission, and Queensland's guidance is to do the work in a yard or slipway with a compliant wash-down system. If you want an in-water clean you ask Biosecurity Queensland, the environment department or Maritime Safety Queensland first, depending on where the boat is. Tributyltin paints have been banned on vessels under 25 m since 1989. None of this is new, but it is why the yard charges an environmental levy and why the diver with a scraper is not a shortcut.

## Timing

- **Days out.** Modern coatings overcoat in six hours and can be launched eight hours after the final coat at 23 °C, so the paint is a two-day job. Propspeed, anodes and drying make it three to five. Bundle an engine service or the annual list and GCCM's guide says one to two weeks.
- **Lead time.** The Boat Works asks for four to six weeks' notice. Demand jumps as the warm months approach, and the yards run hardstand specials through winter for exactly that reason.
- **The boat show.** Sanctuary Cove International Boat Show runs 20 to 23 May 2027. Yards and trades are flat out in the weeks before it.
- **Launch window.** Micron One must be immersed within one to three months of painting. If other work will keep her on the hard, paint last, or use Micron Extra 2, which tolerates 12 months out.

## What to check before you sign

- The lift is included both ways, and the number of hardstand days is written down.
- The paint brand, grade and litres are named. "Or similar" is not a grade.
- Propspeed, anodes and the polish are separate lines with prices, not "as required".
- Someone is inspecting seacocks, shaft seals and skin fittings, and photographing what they find.
- The relaunch date is booked, not "when it's done".
