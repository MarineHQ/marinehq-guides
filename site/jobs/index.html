About Marine HQ

Who you would be working for

Marine HQ looks after motor yachts for their owners. We are based at The Boat Works in Coomera on the Gold Coast and work across the Gold Coast, Sydney and the Whitsundays. We manage and maintain yachts from 40 to 120 ft, supply crew and relief skippers, run deliveries, and give owners the MyYacht app so every job, photo and document is in one place.

We are a small, hands-on team. When work lands we need good people we can call on, so register your interest even if the timing is not right yet and we will keep you on the list.
