MHQ-JB-001 · Sydney, NSW

Commercial skippers, Sydney Harbour

We are building a bench of commercial skippers we can call on in Sydney, from early November 2026. Work is mostly four-hour event and private charter blocks on a commercial passenger vessel, plus the occasional vessel move. Casual, as needed, with more work expected over summer as further commercial vessels come on.

What we are looking for

  • Current commercial skipper qualification for passenger vessels on Sydney Harbour
  • Able to take short-notice work, ideally with 24-hour turnaround
  • Happy to be a preferred skipper or a back-up who learns the boat
  • Professional, presentable and good with guests

MHQ-JB-002 · Sydney, NSW

Deckhands, Sydney Harbour

We also need reliable deckhands in Sydney to crew alongside our skippers, from early November 2026. The commercial passenger vessel runs with a minimum crew of two, so every skipper job needs a deckhand. Work is mostly four-hour event and private charter blocks, casual and as needed, with more expected over summer.

What we are looking for

  • Deck experience on charter, commercial or passenger vessels preferred; keen newcomers with the right attitude considered
  • Current first aid and relevant sea safety certificates
  • Able to take short-notice work, ideally with 24-hour turnaround
  • Professional, presentable and good with guests
Apply for MHQ-JB-002

How to apply

  1. Email crew@marinehq.com.au with your CV attached.
  2. Put the job code in the subject line, for example MHQ-JB-001 - Commercial Skipper - Your Name. The Apply buttons do this for you.
  3. Include your mobile, tickets held and when you are available.

Applying for more than one role? Send one email per code. Not seeing the right role? Join the Marine HQ crew register and we will call when something fits.
