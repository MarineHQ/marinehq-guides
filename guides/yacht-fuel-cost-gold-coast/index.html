Yacht fuel cost: what a 70–80 ft luxury yacht burns (2026)

What Sunseeker, Azimut and Pershing motor yachts of 70 to 80 ft burn at slow, fast and top speed, and what that costs at October 2026 diesel prices.

Updated 10 October 2026·8 min read·Gold Coast, Queensland

A 70 to 80 ft luxury motor yacht burns about 50 to 70 litres an hour at 10 to 12 knots, and 330 to 490 litres an hour at 28 knots. At the October 2026 diesel price of $3.24 a litre, that is $162 to $221 an hour slow and $1,056 to $1,575 an hour fast. Speed is the biggest thing you control, and this guide shows what it costs, boat by boat, using published figures for Azimut, Pershing and Sunseeker.

Key facts — October 2026

  • Diesel — $3.19 a litre for members, $3.24 for non-members, at the Royal Queensland Yacht Squadron on 1 October
  • 71 ft Azimut S7 — about 245 L/h at 24.8 knots, 326 L/h at 28.9 knots
  • Pershing GTX80 — about 68 L/h at 10 knots, 439 L/h at 28 knots
  • 74 ft Sunseeker 74 Sport Yacht — about 50 L/h at 12 knots, 486 L/h at 28 knots (builder’s figures)
  • Per nautical mile — slowing to 10 or 12 knots cuts the fuel cost per mile by more than half on the Pershing and the Sunseeker

A month earlier, on 3 September, the same club listed diesel at $2.79 to $2.84 a litre. That is about 14% more in four weeks. Every dollar figure below uses $3.24 a litre, the non-member price, so it is the cautious end. Ask your own fuel dock for today’s price, and date any number you rely on.

The 76 Yacht’s listing gives a 10 knot range of 500 nautical miles but no burn figure at that speed, so there is no slow-speed row for it. Above about 80 ft we found no published burn figure we could trust; ask the builder for the fuel curve for your engines.

Three things stand out.

  • Slow is a different cost entirely. The Pershing at 10 knots costs $221 an hour. At 28 knots it costs $1,423, over six times as much, to cover 2.8 times the distance.
  • The last few knots cost the most. The Azimut S7 burns 140 more litres an hour going from 28.9 to 36.3 knots, and the Sunseeker 74 costs $256 an hour more at 34 knots than at 28.
  • Your boat will not match these. Engines, load, weather, sea state and hull condition all move every number.

Slow versus fast: what 100 hours costs

The same 100 hours underway on each boat, at the lowest and highest speeds published for it.

Yacht 100 hours at the lower speed 100 hours at the higher speed The difference
Pershing GTX80 (10 and 28 knots) $22,077 $142,271 $120,194
Sunseeker 74 Sport Yacht (12 and 28 knots, builder’s figures) $16,200 $157,464 $141,264
Azimut S7 (24.8 and 28.9 knots) $79,353 $105,599 $26,246
Sunseeker 76 Yacht (23 and 31 knots, builder’s figures) $93,960 $194,400 $100,440

A hundred hours at 28 knots is not a hundred hours at the same distance. At 28 knots the Pershing covers about 2,800 nautical miles in 100 hours; at 10 knots it covers 1,000. Fuel is the cost of the hour, which is why the cost per mile in the tables above is the fairer comparison.

Budget trap

Most owners quote a fuel figure from a brochure or a broker. Ask which speed it was measured at. The number at 10 knots and the number at 28 knots can differ by more than a factor of six.

How to work out your own number

You need three things: the litres an hour at the speed you will run, the hours underway, and the price a litre.

  1. Litres an hour. Ask the builder or your engineer for the fuel curve for your engines, or look up a published sea trial for your model.
  2. Hours underway. Count only the engine hours, not the time at the dock.
  3. Price a litre. Use today’s price from your fuel dock, and add a margin because the price has moved this spring.

For example, a Pershing GTX80 on a two-hour run at 28 knots: 439 litres an hour × 2 hours × $3.24 = $2,845. The same two hours at 10 knots cover about a third of the distance and cost 68 × 2 × $3.24 = $441.

Two things the tables do not include. The generator burns fuel at anchor and overnight, and a speed-and-burn table says nothing about a night at a mooring. And a hull with growth on it burns more at the same speed; our antifouling guide covers how often the Gold Coast’s warm water makes that necessary.

Where fuel money goes

  • Speed, not distance. The Pershing’s cost per mile at 28 knots is more than double its cost at 10 knots, and the Sunseeker 74’s is about four times.
  • Price timing. Diesel rose about 14% between September and October 2026. A trip priced a month ago is not the trip you pay for now.
  • Filling from empty. A full tank is a five-figure cheque on a boat this size. Pay attention to when you fill, not only how much.
  • A fouled hull. A hull with growth burns more fuel at the same speed, so part of the antifoul pays for itself.
  • Generator and hotel loads. Air conditioning and a generator running at a weekend anchorage add litres the speed tables do not show.

Questions owners ask

How much fuel does a 70 to 80 ft luxury motor yacht use per hour?

It depends almost entirely on speed. Yachting magazine’s test of the 71 ft Azimut S7 recorded 64.7 US gallons an hour (245 litres) at 24.8 knots and 86.1 gallons (326 litres) at 28.9 knots. Its test of the Pershing GTX80 recorded 18 gallons an hour (68 litres) at 10 knots and about 116 gallons (439 litres) at 28 knots. At $3.24 a litre that is between $221 and $1,423 an hour.

How much does it cost to fill a luxury yacht's fuel tanks?

Multiply the tank size by the pump price. At $3.24 a litre, the Azimut S7’s 1,004 US gallon tanks (about 3,801 litres) cost about $12,314 to fill from empty, and the Pershing GTX80’s 1,347 US gallons (about 5,099 litres) cost about $16,521. Most owners never fill from empty, but it shows what a full range is worth.

Is it cheaper to run a luxury motor yacht slowly?

Per nautical mile, yes, by a wide margin. The Pershing GTX80 costs about $22 a nautical mile at 10 knots and $51 at 28 knots. The builder’s figures for the Sunseeker 74 Sport Yacht give about $14 a mile at 12 knots and $56 at 28 knots. You get there sooner at speed, but you pay for the time you save.

How much fuel does a 74 ft Sunseeker use?

Builder’s figures listed by YachtBuyer for the Sunseeker 74 Sport Yacht with twin MAN V12-1550 engines are 50 litres an hour at 12 knots, 486 litres at 28 knots and 565 litres at 34 knots. At $3.24 a litre that is $162, $1,575 and $1,831 an hour. These are not independent sea trials and the page does not state the conditions, so treat them as a guide and ask Sunseeker or your engineer for the fuel curve for your own boat.

What is the diesel price on the Gold Coast?

No Gold Coast marina we found publishes a pump price. The nearest published price is the Royal Queensland Yacht Squadron’s in Brisbane: premium diesel at $3.19 a litre for members and $3.24 for non-members on 1 October 2026, up from $2.79 to $2.84 on 3 September. Ask your marina’s fuel dock for today’s price before you budget a trip.

How do I work out what a trip will cost in fuel?

Take the litres an hour at the speed you will run, multiply by the hours underway, then multiply by the price a litre. A Pershing GTX80 at 28 knots burns about 439 litres an hour; a two-hour run is about 878 litres, or about $2,845 at $3.24. The same two hours at 10 knots cost about $441 but cover only 20 nautical miles instead of 56. Add the generator if it will run, because it burns fuel at anchor too.

Why do published fuel figures differ between boats of the same length?

Engines, hull shape, weight and what was aboard on test day all change the result. Yachting magazine ran the Azimut S7 off Cannes in early morning, relatively calm water, with load not stated; the Pershing top-speed run was at half load with trim assist off. Treat any figure as a guide to the size of the bill, and ask the builder or your engineer for the curve for your own engines.
